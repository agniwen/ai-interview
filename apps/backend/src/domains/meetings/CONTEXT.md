# Meetings

Meetings owns Meeting Buddy capture, processing, access, and intelligence independently of the recruiting workflow it may reference.

## Language

**Meeting Recruiting Context**:
The optional link from a Meeting Session to recruiting material owned by Candidate Lifecycle.
_Avoid_: Candidate ownership, interview round

**Meeting Processing Run**:
One idempotent attempt to derive transcript or intelligence artifacts from a Meeting Session recording.
_Avoid_: Queue job, worker execution

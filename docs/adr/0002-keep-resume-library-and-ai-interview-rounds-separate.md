# Keep resume library and AI interview rounds separate

The resume library is the candidate roster, while the AI interview surface is round-oriented. A resume record can exist without any AI interview round, and AI interview list/detail screens should use round identity when the user is acting on a scheduled interview attempt rather than on the candidate profile.

## Consequences

Candidate and resume edits belong to the resume library. Round operations such as link copying, reset, reports, recordings, and round status belong to the AI interview round surface.

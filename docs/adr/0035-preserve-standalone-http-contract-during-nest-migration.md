# Preserve the standalone HTTP contract during the Nest migration

The new `apps/backend` Nest 12 service will replace the independently deployable behavior of `apps/server` while preserving its external `/api/**` HTTP contract, including routes, methods, validation behavior, status semantics, cookies and CORS, redirects, uploads, streams, downloads, health checks, and readiness checks. Error response bodies are the one intentional breaking exception: Nest-owned endpoints adopt the Nest 12 standard exception envelope and stable machine-readable error codes instead of preserving Hono payload shapes. Its internal structure may use Nest modules, controllers, providers, guards, pipes, interceptors, and exception filters; Hono RPC type inference is explicitly outside the compatibility boundary and will be replaced separately by a schema or OpenAPI-derived client contract.
